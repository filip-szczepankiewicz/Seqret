What is simulated

A 3D analytic phantom (ellipsoids with tissue T1, T2, proton density, fat fraction and ADC at roughly 3 T values), cut along the slice given by the excitation (position = RF frequency offset / slice gradient, thickness = RF bandwidth / slice gradient, averaged over five depths), is sampled at the k-space positions and times of the selected volume's ADC samples, using the Fourier transforms of the shapes. Included: T1 steady state for the flip angle and TR, inversion and saturation pulses, T2 and T2′ decay (T2* blurring), six-peak fat with its chemical shift through the true sample timing, spin-echo refocusing, diffusion weighting from the actual gradient waveform (b-value per shot), an air sphere's field, optional residual eddy currents (illustrative exponential model: the signal is taken at the actual k-space position and placed at the nominal one), and noise scaled with the bandwidth. Not included: spin history beyond a steady state, realistic slice profiles, flow, measured eddy-current behaviour, coil sensitivities and g-factor noise. Lines that were not acquired are computed from the same model, as an ideal parallel-imaging or partial-Fourier reconstruction would restore them.

System specifications

Nerve stimulation by the SAFE model, for Siemens systems

Peripheral (PNS) and cardiac (CNS) nerve stimulation are predicted with the SAFE model (Hebrank and Gebhardt, ISMRM 2000) as implemented in safe_pns_prediction. The parameters of a gradient system are confidential and not included: load them from the system's MP_GPA_*.asc or MP_GradSys_*.asc file, or type them in. They are kept only in this browser. The prediction may be inaccurate; interpret it with care.
